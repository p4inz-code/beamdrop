        Quick Guide & About BeamDrop
        
      
      
      
      
        
      
          College / Office PC has a strict firewall?
          
      Go to Connect and click Fix Firewall, or turn on Windows Hotspot and connect your phone directly to your PC's Wi-Fi.
      
        
      
        
      
          Phone and PC are on different Wi-Fi networks?
          
      Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi, or connect your phone to your PC's Mobile Hotspot.
      
        
      
        
      
          Video won't play in phone browser (MKV / AVI)?
          
      Tap Open in VLC on your phone or tap Download to save it at full Wi-Fi speed.
